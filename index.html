俱乐部规则Club Rules

  • 赛程每周一自动更新。The schedule updates automatically on Mondays.
  • 每周一场和每周两场交替进行。Weeks alternate between one game and two games.
  • 每周一场时为竞技赛；周六加周日时，周六竞技、周日休闲；工作日加周末时，工作日休闲、周末竞技。一周从周一到周日。One game in a week is competitive. Saturday + Sunday means Saturday competitive, Sunday casual. Weekday + weekend means the weekday game casual, the weekend game competitive. A week runs Monday to Sunday.
  • 每三个月打一场正式比赛，之后重新选队和选队长。Every three months the league plays an official game, after which new teams and captains are selected.
